Our Story

A Legacy Built on Excellence

Founded in 2006 in Dubai, LuxEstate emerged with a singular mission: to bridge visionary investors with the world's most exceptional properties. What began as a boutique consultancy has grown into a globally recognized real estate powerhouse with offices across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, and Singapore.

Our team of 200+ expert consultants brings local depth and global perspective to every transaction — whether it's a first investment apartment or a multi-portfolio acquisition worth hundreds of millions.

We've facilitated over $4.2 billion in real estate transactions, earning the trust of heads of state, Fortune 500 CEOs, and private investors from 60+ countries.
