Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?
Yes, foreigners can purchase property in designated freehold areas in Dubai with full ownership rights. Popular freehold zones include Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, Business Bay, and DIFC. Our team handles all legal documentation and DLD registration processes on your behalf.
What is the minimum investment for UAE Golden Visa through property?
As of 2024, you can qualify for a 10-year UAE Golden Visa by investing a minimum of AED 2 million (~$545,000 USD) in real estate. The property can be mortgaged, but your equity must meet the threshold. Our advisors can identify qualifying properties and guide you through the entire visa application process.
What fees are involved when buying property in the UAE?
Standard buying costs in Dubai include: Dubai Land Department (DLD) transfer fee of 4%, a registration trustee fee (~AED 4,000), and agent commission of 2%. For off-plan properties, the DLD fee is often 0–2% as developers sometimes cover this. We provide a full cost breakdown before you commit to any transaction.
Do I need to be present in Dubai to complete the purchase?
No — many of our international clients complete purchases remotely. We facilitate virtual property tours, digital document signing (Power of Attorney), and remote DLD registration. You can own your Dubai property without a single flight, though we always welcome in-person visits for major decisions.
What rental yields can I expect from Dubai investment properties?
Dubai offers some of the world's highest rental yields — typically 5–9% annually depending on location and property type. There is no income tax, capital gains tax, or property tax in the UAE. Furnished short-term rentals on platforms like Airbnb can achieve 10–15% yields in prime locations. Our investment team provides detailed ROI projections for every property.
Can LuxEstate help with property management after purchase?
Absolutely. Our Property Management division handles tenant sourcing, lease management, maintenance coordination, and financial reporting. We offer full management packages starting from 5% of annual rental income — ensuring your asset performs without any day-to-day involvement from you.
