Property Description

Presenting a masterpiece of elevated living — the Marina Skyline Penthouse occupies the entire 48th floor of one of Dubai Marina's most iconic residential towers. With 3,800 sq ft of meticulously designed interiors and a sweeping 1,200 sq ft private terrace, this property redefines ultra-luxury living.

The double-height living area is framed by floor-to-ceiling glass, offering unobstructed 270° panoramas of the marina, Palm Jumeirah, and the Arabian Gulf beyond. Every surface has been finished with the finest Italian marble, custom millwork, and bespoke hardware selected from Europe's premium ateliers.

The master suite is a sanctuary unto itself — featuring a private lounge area, walk-in dressing room, and a spa-caliber en-suite bathroom with a freestanding bath positioned to capture the marina vistas. Three additional bedrooms, each with en-suite facilities, complete the private quarters.

The private rooftop terrace houses an infinity-edge plunge pool, outdoor kitchen, and dedicated entertainment lounge — perfect for the discerning entertainer. This penthouse represents an unrivalled opportunity to acquire a trophy asset in Dubai's most celebrated address.
